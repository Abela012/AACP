Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026 · AACP (AI-powered Advertising and Creator Platform)

1. Who we are

AACP (“we”, “us”) operates a marketplace connecting businesses with advertisers and creators. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your choices. Contact: privacy@aacp.io.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, username, role, profile photo, verification documents (if provided).
  • Authentication: session identifiers via Clerk; optional TikTok Login Kit sign-in.
  • TikTok Login Kit: when you sign in with TikTok, we request only the user.info.basic scope (e.g. open_id, display name, avatar). We do not receive your TikTok password.
  • Social connections (optional): tokens and profile metadata when you connect Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok for analytics—scoped to what you authorize.
  • Transactions: wallet/coin activity and payment references (processed by Chapa or similar providers).
  • Usage: logs, device/browser type, IP address (masked where possible), and support messages.

3. How we use data

  • Create and secure your account; enforce roles (business owner, advertiser, admin).
  • Match campaigns, deliver messaging, analytics, and AI-assisted recommendations.
  • Process payments and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with law and respond to valid requests.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process data based on contract performance (providing the service), legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent (optional social connections), and legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Infrastructure and auth providers (e.g. hosting, Clerk, MongoDB).
  • Payment processors (e.g. Chapa).
  • Social platforms you explicitly connect (TikTok, Meta) under their APIs.
  • Authorities when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention and security

We retain data while your account is active and as needed for legal, tax, or dispute purposes. We use encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, and industry-standard practices. No method is 100% secure; report concerns to security@aacp.io.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data, and object to certain processing. Use in-app settings, our data deletion page, or email privacy@aacp.io.

8. Children

AACP is not directed to users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries where our providers operate. We use appropriate safeguards where required.

10. Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes may be notified by email or in-app notice.
